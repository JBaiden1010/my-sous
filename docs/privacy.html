Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

My Sous is a cooking companion for students and beginners, made by Jeremy Baiden in Ottawa, Canada. This page explains what information the app keeps and why. In short: your kitchen and personal details are private, only what you post in the Community is public, there are no ads, and your data is never sold.

Using My Sous as a guest

If you continue as a guest, everything you enter stays in your own browser on your device. Nothing is sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

Signing in

You can sign in with Google. When you do, we receive your name and email address from that provider. We use them only to create your account, show your display name next to Community posts, and let you sign in again. Your email is never shown to other people.

What we store for signed-in cooks

  • Private to you: your profile (name, cooking goal, skill level, language, and optional age, height and weight), your kitchen ingredients and equipment, your cookbook and saved recipes, your cooking history, ratings and notes, and your shopping list.
  • Public in the Community: tips, recipe feedback and star ratings, improvement suggestions, tutorial links and uploaded videos, shared recipes, and "helpful" marks. These appear with your display name.
  • Reports: if you report a post, the report is visible only to moderators.

Age, height and weight are optional and used only to estimate nutrition targets inside the app. They are never shown to anyone else.

How we use it

Only to run the app: showing you recipes that match your kitchen, saving your progress across devices, and running the Community. We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with advertisers.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored with Supabase in Canada (Central), protected by access rules so that only you can read your private data. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Your choices

  • Edit or clear your profile details at any time in Settings.
  • Delete any Community post you made.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Privacy. This permanently removes your account, your saved data, your Community posts and your uploaded videos.

Children

My Sous is meant for students and adults. If you are under the age required to consent to data processing where you live, please use guest mode or ask a parent or guardian.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: mysous.feedback@gmail.com.
